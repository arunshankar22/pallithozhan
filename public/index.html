pallithozhan

Official school portal for Balar Malar Tamil School (NSW) Inc.

Purpose of the Application

The pallithozhan app (meaning School Companion) is the official education and administrative portal for Balar Malar Tamil School (NSW) Inc. It is designed to streamline academic progress and school communication, offering tools for digital attendance management, homework tracking, term progress reports, newsletter distribution, and student milestone points.

Data Privacy & Google OAuth Transparency

We respect your data privacy. When using Google Sign-In, the pallithozhan app requests access to your basic Google profile details (specifically your email address, full name, and avatar picture URL). This information is accessed solely to authenticate your identity, secure your login, and link your account to your designated role (Teacher, Volunteer, Parent, or Student) in our school system. No private information or OAuth data is ever shared with third parties or used for tracking.
