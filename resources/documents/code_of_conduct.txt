Balar Malar Parramatta - Staff and Student Code of Conduct

1. Staff & Volunteer Information Handling Protocol:
- Device Security: Staff must access the portal using personal, password-protected user accounts. Shared accounts are strictly prohibited. Always log out when using public/shared computers.
- No Downloading PII: Staff should never download raw student/parent spreadsheets containing phone numbers, emails, or DOBs to their personal local hard drives. Access records directly through the secure portal interface.
- Printed attendance logs or registration sheets must not be left unattended in classrooms. Shred or securely dispose of printouts at the end of the term.
- WWC Checks: Only verified administrators can approve and verify Working With Children status.
- Email Safety: When emailing multiple parents, staff must use BCC (Blind Carbon Copy) to prevent exposing email addresses in bulk mailouts.

2. Student Guidelines and Discipline:
- Punctuality: Students must arrive 10 minutes before class starts.
- Language: Students are encouraged to communicate primarily in Tamil during school hours.
- Mobile Devices: Mobile phones must be turned off or kept on silent in school bags during lessons.
- Respect: Bullying, harassment, or disrespectful behavior towards teachers, volunteers, or fellow students will not be tolerated and may lead to suspension.
- Cleanliness: Students are responsible for maintaining classroom cleanliness and disposing of waste in recycling/general waste bins.
- Pick-up Safety: Parents must sign students out at the designated pick-up zones. Students are not allowed to leave the premises unaccompanied unless written authorization is provided.
