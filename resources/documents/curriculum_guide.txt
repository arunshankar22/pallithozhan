Balar Malar Parramatta - School Curriculum Guide (KG to Year 9)

1. Kindergarten (KG) & Year 1 (Primary Literacy):
- Goal: Familiarize children with basic Tamil sounds, spoken vocabulary, and alphabet recognition.
- Content: Tamil Vowels (உயிரெழுத்துக்கள் - 12 letters) and Tamil Consonants (மெய்யெழுத்துக்கள் - 18 letters).
- Focus: Simple coloring workbooks, singing Tamil kids rhymes (e.g. நிலா நிலா ஓடி வா), identifying animals, colors, and fruits in Tamil.
- Expected Outcome: Ability to read and write all 30 primary letters and speak short sentences.

2. Year 2 & Year 3 (Word Building & Alphabetic Writing):
- Goal: Learn letter combinations, reading short paragraphs, and simple moral values.
- Content: Ayutha Ezhuthu (ஃ), building Tamil words combining consonants and vowels (உயிர்மெய் எழுத்துக்கள்).
- Focus: Introduction to Athichudi (ஆத்திசூடி) verses by Avvaiyar with meanings.
- Expected Outcome: Ability to read simple stories, write spelling lists, and comprehend short stories (like Panchatantra fables).

3. Year 4 & Year 5 (Intermediate Grammar & Sentences):
- Goal: Structure sentences, differentiate noun/verb classes, and write short paragraphs.
- Content: Parts of speech (பெயர்ச்சொல், வினைச்சொல்), tenses (இறந்த காலம், நிகழ்காலம், எதிர்காலம்).
- Focus: Studying Thirukkural (திருக்குறள்) verses with moral stories.
- Expected Outcome: Writing weekly diary entries, constructing grammatically correct questions, and reading children's books independently.

4. Year 6 & Year 7 (Literature & Conversational Tamil):
- Goal: Vocabulary extension, public speaking, and understanding Tamil history.
- Content: Tales of Tenali Raman, Sangam Literature fables adapted for children, and historical essays.
- Focus: Writing structured paragraphs, advanced verbs, and participating in debates.
- Expected Outcome: Delivering a 2-minute speech in Tamil, reading complex storybooks, and writing descriptive essays.

5. Year 8 & Year 9 (Advanced Prose & Cultural Studies):
- Goal: Literary appreciation, advanced grammar conventions, and Tamil heritage.
- Content: Ancient Tamil dynasties (Chera, Chola, Pandya), trade routes, temple architectures, and formal essay writing.
- Focus: Reading Tamil prose, writing multi-paragraph reports, and active participation in school newsletters.
- Expected Outcome: Structuring research papers in Tamil, reading news prints, and translating complex English passages to Tamil.
